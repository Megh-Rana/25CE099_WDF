Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the StudentHub portal.

How do I register for an account?

Go to the Register page, fill in your personal, academic, and security details, and click Register. Your enrollment number will be used for profile verification.

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

Currently, you must contact the campus administrator at the Faculty of Technology and Engineering to reset your password.

Can I register for multiple campus events?

Yes, you can register for as many active events as you want, provided there are no scheduling conflicts.

How do I submit feedback about the portal?

Log in to your account, navigate to the Feedback page, fill out the feedback form, and submit your suggestions.
