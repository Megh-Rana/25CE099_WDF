About StudentHub

Built for students, by students — simplifying campus life at CHARUSAT.

Our Mission

StudentHub aims to be the single digital gateway for all student activities at CHARUSAT. From managing your academic profile to registering for campus events, our portal brings everything under one roof — accessible, secure, and easy to use.

About the Institution

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a premier institution located in Changa, Gujarat. The Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE) offers cutting-edge programs in Computer Science, IT, and related fields, preparing students for the challenges of the modern tech industry.
